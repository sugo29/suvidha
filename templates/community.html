Learn & FAQs

Understanding civic data and utilities.

How is ward data collected?

Data is aggregated from official meter readings and anonymised to ensure privacy.

What does "Supply Stress" mean?

It indicates high demand on the infrastructure or frequent interruptions in service.

Do challenges affect my bill?

No. Challenges are voluntary and strictly non-monetary initiatives to support the grid.
